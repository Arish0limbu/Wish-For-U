Meri pyari wifu,

Janmadinko dherai dherai shubhakamana, mero sadharan dinharulai pani khas banaune manche. Aasha cha, yo barsa timilai muskuraune naya karan, bholiko lagi utsahit banaune sapana, ra timi le aru lai dine sabai maya farkera lyaos.

Hami le saath bitayeka harek sano pal ko lagi ma aabhari chhu: hamro haaso, shanta dinharu, ra mero manparne sochharuma feri aaune samjhanaharu. Timi jasto chau, tyastai bhayera mero sansar ajhai nyano banauchau.

Timi jasti anauthi chau, tyastai sadhai rahanu. Aaja ra aaune harek adhyayma ma sadhai timro lagi khusi le taali bajaunechhu.

Janmadinko shubhakamana, mero maya.

Sadhai timrai, Arish ♥
